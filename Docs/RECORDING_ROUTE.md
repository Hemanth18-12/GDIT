# 3-minute recording route

Settings before recording: 1920 x 1080, Graphics High, Weather High, Difficulty Easy (so the clock is relaxed),
On-screen controls "In fights". Start from **New Game**. Have the choices below ready so Kishore turns.

| Time | Where | What to do | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Main menu → New Game | Click NEW GAME | Loading screen: dawn courtyard picture, gold bar, "CHAPTER 1", "Dawn in 02:35", a tip |
| 0:12–0:30 | Courtyard, 3:10 AM | Walk to the Pujari, press E, read two lines, skip the rest | Moonlit courtyard in drizzle, oil lamps, the Dawn Clock at the top |
| 0:30–0:50 | Courtyard | Talk to Meera. Choose **3 "It was dark."**, then **1 Gentle** | The "tell", the choice, trust +1 |
| 0:50–1:05 | Courtyard | Sprint after the runner for about ten seconds, pick up the token | The chase panel, the runner, porter token No. 17 |
| 1:05–1:25 | Storage hall | G for the flashlight, inspect one clue, light the lamps: Sunrise, Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Dusk | Dark hall, battery, the hall lighting up lamp by lamp |
| 1:25–1:38 | Storage hall | Talk to the Pujari, choose **1 Trust her**; press B, answer 2, 1, 2 | Meera's confession, the deduction board |
| 1:38–1:55 | Outer shrine | Crouch at a bell thread, press E three times in the green zone; press K and slide the ruler | Heavy rain, tripwire ring, forensic ruler |
| 1:55–2:10 | Sun Wall | Talk to the Pujari (skip), press E at the wall, 1 2 3 4, Enter, alternate A and D | The 90 s timer, the hand-wheel bar, the wall opening |
| 2:10–2:22 | Jungle ruins | Crouch in the grass, throw one pebble with T | Vision cones, the detection meter, the storm, lightning |
| 2:22–2:48 | Brazier courtyard | Block a white glint, dodge a red one, three attacks, G flash, knock a brazier with E, finish with E | Fight HUD, on-screen buttons, Kishore turning on Bhaskar, phase 2 |
| 2:48–2:55 | The ford | Sprint, alternate A and D, press E in the green zone | The pursuit and the grapple |
| 2:55–3:00 | Courtyard | Return (automatic), talk to the Pujari | First light, the storm cleared, ending card: DAWN LIGHT |

Tips: skip dialogue lines with Space once the first words are heard; if a section takes too long, cut in editing
rather than rushing the play. Record system audio so the voice, rain and music stems are captured.
