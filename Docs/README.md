# Legacy of the Temple

A short third-person thriller set around a fictional Indian temple town. Five treasures have been stolen from the temple storeroom the night before the festival. You are Aryan, an investigator, and you have until dawn to take them back and find out who opened the door from the inside.

Everything in the story is fiction: the village of Suryapur, the treasures and the characters. No real deity is shown, stolen or carried.

## How to run it
- **Mac:** open `Builds/LegacyOfTheTemple.app`, then choose **New Game**.
- **Unity:** open the `My project` folder in Unity 6000.6.8f1, open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Dev/MainMenu.unity` and press Play.
- Requirements: macOS on Apple silicon or Intel, 8 GB of memory, a screen of 1280 x 720 or larger. Sound must be on: the game uses sound to guide you.

## How to play
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Walk (hold Shift to run) |
| Mouse | Look |
| C | Crouch (harder to see) |
| E | Grab, turn, push, place, talk, get in and out of the jeep |
| H | Guide and three hints for the puzzle you are standing at |
| B | Evidence board: what you know, and naming the inside man |
| J / M | Journal and map |
| Esc | Pause, settings, controls |
| Left mouse | Strike (three hits make a combo) |
| R | Heavy strike |
| V | Dodge roll |
| Q (hold) | Block; press it just as a blow lands to parry |
| F | Finish a staggered or beaten robber |
| In the jeep | W S drive, A D steer, Space brake, L lights, G horn |

A game controller also works (left stick to move, south button to act).

## What happens
1. Walk through the old stone gate. The temple appears.
2. Talk to Pujari Ananda.
3. **Bazaar:** follow a courier with a ringing bag without being seen. Corner him in a dyer's yard.
4. **Storeroom:** rebuild the break-in from a broken pot, a lantern, an oil stain and a lock plate.
5. **Vaults:** push stone blocks onto floor plates, then turn three stone discs to match the light on the floor.
6. **Stepwell:** fill the tank with two sluice wheels, ride the raft, and drain it back to the carved mark.
7. **Sun Wall:** turn four bronze mirrors and push the chariot wheel so two sunbeams reach the wall.
8. **River road:** chase Bhaskar by jeep to the ruins, survive two waves, then defeat him.
9. Bring the treasures back to the priest.

Every place ends with a short fight. The Dawn Clock at the top of the screen counts down to sunrise. If you run out of time in a chapter you retry from its start; finishing late gives a different ending.

## Score
Each place gives a grade (S, A, B or C) for solving its puzzle without hints, staying unseen, speed, taking little damage, recovering the treasure and fight combos. The ending shows every grade and the overall one. Naming the inside man correctly before the last place makes the final fight shorter.

## Settings
Graphics preset (Low, Medium, High, Ultra; High is the default), fullscreen, mouse speed, invert look, difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard), master, music, effects and voice volume, subtitles, text size, high contrast.
