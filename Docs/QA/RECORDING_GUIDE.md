# Legacy of the Temple — Video Recording Guide (macOS)

This guide explains how to capture gameplay video of "LEGACY OF THE TEMPLE" using native macOS Screen Recording (`Cmd + Shift + 5`) for crisp visuals, 60 FPS, and clear audio.

---

## 1. Quick Capture Shortcuts in Game
- **Cinematic Showcase Mode**: Click **"SHOWCASE TOUR"** on the Main Menu (or `Tools > Legacy > Showcase` in the Unity Editor). This automatically runs an unhurried, multi-scene camera tour across all 5 environments with lower-third character introduction banners. Press **`[Esc]`** anytime to exit.
- **Photo Mode (Hide/Restore UI)**: Press **`[P]`** anytime during real-time gameplay to instantly toggle off all UI (HUD, objective panel, interaction prompts, and controls hint bar). Press **`[P]`** again to restore the UI.
- **How to Play & Mission Brief**: Press **`[H]`** to display the full controls diagram and mission goals.

---

## 2. Recommended macOS Screen Recording Setup (`Cmd + Shift + 5`)

1. Press **`Cmd + Shift + 5`** to open the macOS screen recording overlay.
2. Under the capture options bar:
   - Choose **"Record Entire Screen"** (or **"Record Selected Window"** and click on `LegacyOfTheTemple.app`).
3. Click **Options**:
   - **Save to**: Desktop or a dedicated folder (e.g., `Docs/QA/Videos/`).
   - **Timer**: 5 Seconds (gives you time to switch focus to the game window and let any cursor settle).
   - **Microphone**: Select your internal or external microphone if adding voiceover commentary, or **None** for pure game audio.
   - **Show Mouse Clicks**: Turn **Off** for cinematic capture.
4. Click **Record**.
5. Switch to the game window. For fullscreen recording, enter fullscreen via macOS green window button or game settings.
6. When finished recording, click the **Stop Recording icon** in the macOS menu bar (circle with a square inside) or press **`Cmd + Control + Esc`**.

---

## 3. Recommended Showcase Sequence for Your Video
1. **Title & Menu**: Capture the Main Menu showing "LEGACY OF THE TEMPLE", "SHOWCASE TOUR", and "NEW GAME".
2. **How to Play & Controls**: Start a New Game or press **`[H]`** to display the keycaps controls map and the 3-line sacred investigation brief.
3. **Showcase Tour**:
   - **Scene 1 (Courtyard Plaza)**: Sunrise skybox, weathered sandstone temple, Investigator Aryan, Pujari Ananda, and Meera in her traditional saree.
   - **Scene 2 (Inner Sanctum)**: Carved stone pillars, brass oil lamps with flickering light, Garbhagriha background shrine, and the 3 empty stands.
   - **Scene 3 (Crime Scene)**: Ransacked outer shrine, broken doorway, muddy footprints, and red silk scrap.
   - **Scene 4 (Puzzle Wall)**: Ancient Surya Chakra carved solar wall with lotus, conch, wheel, and kalash reliefs.
   - **Scene 5 (Jungle Path)**: Moonlit canopy, ruined stone staircase, distant glowing temple, and confrontation with Bhaskar (red scarf) and Kishore (black hood).
4. **Resolution & Playback**: The game runs at native 60 FPS with ACES tonemapping and physically-based URP lighting.
