### Section 2 · Quick knowledge check (Set B)

The same six ideas with a new scene or new numbers. Each question is worth 1 point.

1. A lamp hangs above a clay pot on the floor. You lift the lamp higher. What happens to the pot's shadow on the floor?
   - It gets smaller **(correct)**
   - It gets bigger
   - It stays the same size
   - It moves onto the wall
2. You turn a flat mirror by 10° while a beam of light is reflecting off it. By how much does the reflected beam turn?
   - 5°
   - 10°
   - 20° **(correct)**
   - 40°
3. A wooden raft floats in a stepwell tank. You drain the tank until the water level drops by 1 m. What happens to the raft?
   - It goes down 1 m and still floats **(correct)**
   - It stays where it was
   - It sinks to the bottom
   - It goes down 2 m
4. Four clues were found: a list in neat handwriting, scratches inside a lock, a ledger signed "M." and a temple key ring. What is the best way to name a suspect?
   - Pick whoever was standing nearest
   - Pick the one person that all the clues point to **(correct)**
   - Pick the person with the most money
   - Pick someone at random
5. A dial has 8 stops (0 to 7) and turns only forward, one stop per push. It is at stop 6 and must reach stop 1. How many pushes are needed?
   - 3 **(correct)**
   - 5
   - 7
   - 2
6. The priest says the stolen temple pieces "carry the community's memory". Which reason best explains why they are priceless?
   - The gold in them
   - Their price at a market
   - The history and meaning they hold for the people **(correct)**
   - Only how old they are
7. How sure were you of your answers above? (1 = Guessing … 5 = Very sure)
