Answer key (Set B, 1 point each)

#QuestionCorrect answerWhy
1A lamp hangs above a clay pot on the floor. You lift the lamp higher. What happens to the pot's shadow on the floor?It gets smallerLight travels in straight lines. A higher lamp sends its rays down more steeply, so the shadow on the floor shrinks towards the pot's own size.
2You turn a flat mirror by 10° while a beam of light is reflecting off it. By how much does the reflected beam turn?20°When a mirror turns by an angle, the reflected beam turns by twice that angle (angle in = angle out on both sides). This is why each Sun Wall mirror moves the beam a lot.
3A wooden raft floats in a stepwell tank. You drain the tank until the water level drops by 1 m. What happens to the raft?It goes down 1 m and still floatsA floating raft always rests on the surface; if the water drops 1 m, the raft drops 1 m with it (as in the stepwell).
4Four clues were found: a list in neat handwriting, scratches inside a lock, a ledger signed "M." and a temple key ring. What is the best way to name a suspect?Pick the one person that all the clues point toGood reasoning uses all the evidence together, as on the evidence board: the inside man is the one person every clue points to.
5A dial has 8 stops (0 to 7) and turns only forward, one stop per push. It is at stop 6 and must reach stop 1. How many pushes are needed?36 → 7 → 0 → 1 is 3 pushes: after the last stop the dial goes round to 0 again (like the vault discs).
6The priest says the stolen temple pieces "carry the community's memory". Which reason best explains why they are priceless?The history and meaning they hold for the peopleThe game's message: "Their value isn't money. It's memory." Heritage objects matter for the history and meaning they hold for a community.
