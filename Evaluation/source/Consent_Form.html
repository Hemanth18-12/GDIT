Consent to Participate in a Research Experiment

Study: Legacy of the Temple, an Educational 3D Heritage Mystery Game

By signing below, I confirm that I have read the information sheet and have been informed about the purpose and procedure of this research study, which evaluates player experience, usability, engagement and learning through the educational game "Legacy of the Temple".

The game includes puzzle activities based on observation, reasoning from evidence, light and shadow, reflection, water levels and planning, set in a fictional temple village, along with short knowledge-based questions. It contains short, non-graphic fights. During the session, I will fill in two short forms, play the game, and give feedback about my experience.

I understand that my participation is voluntary. I may stop participating at any time without giving a reason and without any penalty or negative consequences.

I understand that my gameplay data (places reached, hints used and the final score), my form responses and my feedback may be analysed for academic research and project evaluation. Results will be reported in an anonymised or aggregated form. My name and participant ID will not be included in any research results.
